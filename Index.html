ENTERTAINER · Search redesign

Find what you mean.

Search understands the intent behind your words, and ranks results accordingly. The app already parses "pizza near me" into words. What it doesn't do is rank by what those words mean. In testing, that search put a spa ahead of the pizza places, because it's matching text, not understanding intent. This redesign makes the understanding explicit and ranks on it, on top of the merchant-per-outlet repeats and the scope that's never shown.

What changed

  1. Understood intent, shown as such. "Pizza near me" resolves to Pizza · Near you as a visible chip, not silently discarded words. Weak matches drop out, and proximity ranks what's left, so a spa can't outrank the pizza places.
  2. One card per merchant. Offers belong to the merchant. Outlets fold underneath, labelled the way the catalogue is: outlets, not "locations."
  3. Scope widens itself. Naming a far place expands the search instead of returning nothing.
  4. Redeem, not book. A 4-digit PIN in person. Hotels need a call ahead first.
