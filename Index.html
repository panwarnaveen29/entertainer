ENTERTAINER · Search redesign

Find what you mean.

Search understands the intent behind your words, and ranks results accordingly. The app already parses "pizza near me" into words. What it doesn't do is rank by what those words mean. In testing, that search put a spa ahead of the pizza places, because it's matching text, not understanding intent. This redesign ranks on that understanding directly, on top of the merchant-per-outlet repeats and the scope that's never shown.

What changed

  1. Ranked by meaning, not just matched. "Pizza near me" only returns pizza places. Weak matches drop out entirely instead of getting buried lower, and proximity ranks what's left, so a spa can't outrank the pizza places.
  2. One card per merchant. Offers belong to the merchant. Outlets fold underneath, labelled the way the catalogue is: outlets, not "locations."
  3. Scope widens itself. Naming a far place expands the search instead of returning nothing.
  4. Redeem, not book. A 4-digit PIN in person. Hotels need a call ahead first.
